The mines of Eldora

A quiet mining world. An unexpected invasion.

Eldora's people built their lives around the riches hidden beneath the surface. When alien creatures seize the caves, one Miner must recover the treasure, reopen the passages, and make the underground safe again.

A bomb can reveal the way forward—or destroy treasure, remove a power-up, and leave the Miner without an escape.
